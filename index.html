Common questions

What should I bring to a first meeting?

Last year's return, this year's income forms (W-2s, 1099s, K-1s), and any records of deductions. For businesses, bring your bank statements and whatever bookkeeping you have, even if it's a shoebox. We'll tell you what else we need.

Do I need to come to the office?

No. Most clients send documents through the secure client portal and review their return by phone or video. You're welcome in the office any time, though.

How is pricing set?

By the work involved, agreed before we start. A simple individual return costs less than a business return with bookkeeping. You'll have a clear quote up front, not a surprise invoice.

Can you help if I'm behind on filing?

Yes. Catching up on unfiled years and dealing with the resulting notices is a regular part of our work. The sooner we start, the more options you have.

Do you work with businesses outside Dearborn Heights?

Yes. We serve clients across metro Detroit and, through the portal, across Michigan and beyond.
