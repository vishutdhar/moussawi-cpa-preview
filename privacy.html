Privacy and accessibility

Applies to moussawicpa.com. Last updated November 1, 2026.

This website is a brochure for Moussawi & Associates, CPA, at 26169 W Warren St, Dearborn Heights, MI 48127. It exists to tell you what we do and how to reach us. It is not where client work happens.

What this site collects

  • Nothing by default. The site has no accounts, no cookies of its own, and no advertising trackers.
  • The consultation form does not store anything on our servers. When you submit it, it opens a message in your own email app addressed to ali@moussawicpa.com with the details you typed. Sending that message is up to you, and it travels through your email provider and ours like any other email.
  • Please do not send Social Security numbers, account numbers or tax documents through the form or by regular email. Clients exchange documents through the secure client portal, which has its own privacy terms.
  • If we add visitor statistics in future, we will use a cookieless, privacy-first service that does not identify individual visitors, and we will update this page.

How we use what you send

We use your message to reply to you about the services you asked about. We do not sell or share your contact details, and we do not add you to any marketing list.

Client information

Once you become a client, your tax and financial information is handled under our written information security plan, as required of tax professionals by the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act and the FTC Safeguards Rule. Ask us for a copy of our client privacy notice at any time.

Links to other sites

The client portal (SmartVault) and the map directions link are run by other companies with their own privacy practices.

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site. It is built to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA: it works with a keyboard alone, has visible focus, readable contrast, a skip link, and respects reduced-motion settings. If something does not work for you, tell us and we will fix it or help you another way.
